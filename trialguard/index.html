FAQ

How is this different from Kumbak, ReturnMate, Refund Radar or OEPS?

Those focus on returns, are Android-only (Kumbak) or are tied to specific shops. TrialGuard covers subscriptions and online purchases together and drafts the actual cancel or withdrawal request. It also works in any browser.

How is it different from Rocket Money?

There's no bank linking and no upsells. It's just deadlines, reminders and ready-made requests.

What changed in the EU?

Since 19 June 2026, EU online shops must offer a withdrawal function (overview, ECC Finland). TrialGuard helps you keep track of deadlines. It is not legal advice, and whether a purchase can be withdrawn depends on the seller and the product.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, it tracks up to 3 items. Lifetime unlocks unlimited items and receipt forwarding.
