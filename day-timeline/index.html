Your day as a timeline, on every new tab

For people who plan their day in time blocks. Every new Chrome tab shows today's schedule with a line at the current time. Drag tasks onto the timeline and see your Google or Outlook meetings by importing an exported .ics file (a snapshot you re-import to refresh, no live sync). What it does

  • A visual timeline of today with a line showing the current time.
  • Drag a task to set its start time. It snaps to 15 minutes, and every task keeps its own start time.
  • Import any .ics calendar file, read-only.
  • No account. Data stays in your browser (chrome.storage.local).
  • Keyboard accessible, with light and dark mode.
  • No analytics in the extension, and it makes no network requests.
