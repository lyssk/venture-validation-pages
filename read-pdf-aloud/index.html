How it works

  1. Click the extension's icon. Its reader opens in a new tab.
  2. Choose a PDF or drop it on the page. The text is read from the file inside your browser.
  3. Press Play. Change the speed or the voice, jump to a page, or click a sentence to start from it.
  4. Close the tab whenever you like. Open the same PDF later and it offers to continue where you stopped.

Desktop Chrome only. The extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet; leave your email above if you'd like one message when it is.

What it does

  • Reads a PDF aloud using the speech voices already in your browser, one sentence at a time.
  • Highlights the sentence being read, and optionally the word, when the voice reports word positions.
  • Play and pause, stop, previous and next sentence, speed from 0.5× to 2.5×, voice picker, jump to any page, click a sentence to start there.
  • Remembers your voice, speed and, for each PDF, where you stopped. Settings and reading positions are stored locally. Positions are keyed by a file fingerprint; file name and PDF text are not saved.
  • Free, no account, no analytics in the extension. Its only permission is storage.

Voices and your privacy

We receive no data from the extension. The PDF text and saved settings/positions are processed locally; explicitly selected online voices send spoken text to their provider. The extension picks a voice that runs on your device by default. Some browser voices, such as Chrome's "Google …" voices, are online voices: the text being read is sent to Google to make the audio. These are clearly labelled in the voice list, and the extension uses one only if you choose it. Your PDF file itself isn't uploaded to us or anyone else. See the extension privacy policy.

What it doesn't do (yet)

  • No OCR: scanned PDFs that are only pictures of text have nothing to read.
  • Text is read in the order the PDF stores it. Two-column layouts may be read out of order, and page headers and footers are read too.
  • Password-protected PDFs can't be opened.
  • It reads PDFs you open in its own reader, not the PDF already open in a tab.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no paid version and no account.

Does it work offline?

Not tested yet, so we don't claim it. Opening the PDF and reading its text happen in your browser; whether speech works without a connection depends on the voice you use.

Are there other free options?

Yes. Other Chrome extensions also read PDFs aloud, for example Read Aloud and Read PDF Aloud. We don't claim this one is better.
